GETTING STARTED

How it works

Three simple steps to start exploring health information.

1

Ask a question

Enter a clear, general health or wellness question in the chat box. Avoid sharing private details.

2

Review the information

Read the response carefully. AI responses may contain errors and should not be treated as a confirmed diagnosis.

3

Choose a safe next step

Contact a qualified healthcare professional for personal advice, diagnosis, or treatment recommendations.

⚠️ Need urgent medical help?

If someone has severe difficulty breathing, becomes unconscious, has signs of a stroke, or faces another potentially life-threatening emergency, contact local emergency services immediately. In India, dial 112. Do not wait for an AI response.
